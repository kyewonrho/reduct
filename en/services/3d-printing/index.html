REDUCE PRODUCT TECHNOLOGY

3D printing is most useful when the validation objective is defined first.

Printed parts are used for form, assembly, functional testing, fixtures and development aids, with CNC or sheet metal added where precision or strength is required.

Form and assembly validation

Rapid prints help check envelope, interference, assembly sequence and user touchpoints.

Functional prototypes

Material and process are selected around strength, dimensional stability, thermal and friction requirements where applicable.

Development fixtures

Temporary jigs, sensor brackets, assembly aids and test fixtures can be quickly iterated with additive manufacturing.

Hybrid prototype builds

CNC or sheet metal can be combined with printed non-structural parts to shorten the overall development schedule.

Service Area

Based in Songdo, Incheon, we support projects across Korea and work with overseas clients through drawings, 3D CAD, BOM data and remote engineering reviews.
