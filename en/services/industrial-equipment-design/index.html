REDUCE PRODUCT TECHNOLOGY

Equipment design has to balance function, assembly, manufacturing cost and maintenance.

Industrial equipment structure, automation-machine mechanical design, frames, covers and component packaging are developed with manufacturability and cost in mind.

Industrial equipment mechanical design

Machine envelope, frames, covers, moving parts, sensors, motors, electrical packaging and operator access are developed in 3D.

Automation equipment modules

Drive modules, transfer units, brackets, bases and guarding can be developed as modular subassemblies.

Manufacturing process and cost

Machining, sheet metal, fabrication, standard extrusion and purchased components are compared to achieve the required performance efficiently.

Production drawings and OEM sourcing

Drawings and BOMs are prepared for manufacturing, then matched with suppliers by process capability.

Service Area

Based in Songdo, Incheon, we support projects across Korea and work with overseas clients through drawings, 3D CAD, BOM data and remote engineering reviews.
