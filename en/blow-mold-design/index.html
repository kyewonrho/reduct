Blow Mold Design Services

We design parting, pinch-off, neck, venting and cooling structures for blow-molded products and deliver manufacturing-ready design data.

Blow mold engineering scope

  • Parting line and opening direction
  • Pinch-off geometry and flash area
  • Neck and insert structure
  • Venting features
  • Cooling circuit
  • Cavity geometry and machining considerations
  • Ejection and post-processing conditions

Product geometry and molding risk

Blow-molded parts can be sensitive to section changes, corners, neck features and pinch-off conditions. Product geometry is reviewed together with mold structure and forming risk.

Wall distribution

Review abrupt geometry changes and corners that can cause uneven material distribution.

Pinch-off

Review joint strength and flash removal requirements.

Neck structure

Review neck accuracy, inserts and local tooling details.

Cooling & venting

Design heat removal and air escape paths for stable molding.
