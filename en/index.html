PROBLEM

ABOUT

REDUCT is a name built around the idea of Reduce Product Technology. We are a technology consulting and R&D company that analyzes the cost structure of products and industrial equipment and optimizes engineering, development, and manufacturing methods.

We review structure, components, electronic specifications, manufacturing methods, and supply chains at the project level while preserving target performance and quality. Part redesign, OEM sourcing, hardware and firmware development, mechanical engineering, industrial design, and mold design/manufacturing are execution tools for that work.

HOW WE OPTIMIZE

Analyze. Redesign.
Validate.

We do not start by manufacturing. We first identify why the cost exists, redesign the structure, and then validate manufacturability, quality, and supply conditions.

  1. 01

    ANALYZE

    Review structure, parts, specifications, process, and sourcing conditions as one system.

  2. 02

    IDENTIFY

    Separate overspecification, unnecessary complexity, inefficient processes, and supply-related cost drivers.

  3. 03

    REDESIGN

    Redesign part count, geometry, assembly method, electronic specifications, and manufacturing process while maintaining required performance.

  4. 04

    SOURCE

    Match the design with the right CNC, sheet metal, mold, OEM, or injection-molding supply chain.

  5. 05

    VALIDATE

    Validate cost, quality, productivity, and sourcing conditions before execution.

WHERE WE OPTIMIZE

Cost Is Hidden
Across the Product.

Cost is not determined by purchase price alone. It accumulates across structure, parts, electronics, manufacturing processes, and the supply chain.

STRUCTURE

Simplify architecture, fastening, assembly, and unnecessary geometry.

PARTS

Reduce part count, standardize components, review substitutes, and lower machining difficulty.

ELECTRONICS

Optimize PCB, sensors, motors, power, and communication specifications around required functions.

PROCESS

Review CNC, sheet metal, welding, tooling, and molding processes.

SUPPLY CHAIN

Compare and restructure OEM, machining, mold, and molding suppliers.
