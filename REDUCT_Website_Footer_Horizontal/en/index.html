ABOUT

Reduce What
Doesn’t Add Value.

Keep what creates value. Reduce unnecessary cost and inefficiency.

REDUCT is a name built around the idea of Reduce Product Technology. We are a technology consulting and R&D company that analyzes the cost structure of products and industrial equipment and optimizes engineering, development, and manufacturing methods.

We review structure, components, electronic specifications, manufacturing methods, and supply chains at the project level while preserving target performance and quality. Part redesign, OEM sourcing, hardware and firmware development, mechanical engineering, industrial design, and mold design/manufacturing are execution tools for that work.

OUR PRINCIPLE

Same Purpose.
Better Efficiency.

Cost reduction is not simply about using cheaper parts.

Our approach is to eliminate unnecessary specifications, simplify structures, and select better manufacturing methods and suppliers to reduce total cost while preserving product purpose and quality.

HOW WE EXECUTE

Optimization,
Then Execution.

Once the optimization direction is defined, we combine engineering capabilities with an external manufacturing network and implement it in actual products and equipment.

Industrial Equipment & OEM Optimization

We analyze parts used in domestically manufactured industrial equipment, redesign or replace them where appropriate, and compare qualified OEM suppliers to reduce part manufacturing and procurement costs.

Hardware & Firmware Engineering

We develop circuits, PCBs, MCU firmware, sensor, motor, and communication control to the level actually required by the product. Electronic specifications and control architecture are optimized to reduce over-engineering and unnecessary BOM cost.

Hardware & Firmware Development

Mold & Manufacturing Optimization

We review mold architecture based on product structure, target volume, and target cost, then design and manufacture the mold. Injection molding production is coordinated with partner factories suited to the project conditions.

WHERE WE OPTIMIZE

Cost Is Hidden
Across the Product.

Cost is not determined by purchase price alone. It accumulates across structure, part count, electronic specifications, machining processes, tooling, assembly, and the supply chain.

01 / EQUIPMENT OPTIMIZATION

Equipment Cost Reduction

We redesign equipment parts and modules and optimize manufacturing methods and OEM sourcing to reduce equipment build cost and ongoing procurement cost.

02 / PRODUCT OPTIMIZATION

Hardware Specification Optimization

We right-size mechanical, electronic, and firmware specifications to actual functional requirements, reducing over-engineering and unnecessary component cost.

03 / MANUFACTURING OPTIMIZATION

Tooling & Manufacturing Cost

We select tooling and manufacturing methods suited to the product structure and volume, then configure appropriate production conditions through our manufacturing network.
