REDUCE PRODUCT TECHNOLOGY

Mechanical design should be manufacturable, not just visually complete.

3D CAD, structural design, assembly architecture, production drawings, tolerances and DFM are developed with the intended manufacturing process in mind.

3D mechanical and structural design

We build 3D CAD around envelope, component packaging, fastening, assembly sequence and serviceability for new products, redesigns and production transitions.

Production drawings and tolerances

Datum strategy, dimensions, tolerances, materials, finishes and fastening requirements are documented for suppliers while avoiding unnecessary precision that increases cost.

Design for manufacturing

Geometry is adapted for CNC machining, sheet-metal bending, welding, molding or additive manufacturing as appropriate.

Drawing recovery and redesign

Existing drawings or physical parts can be reconstructed into 3D data and improved for replacement or local production.

Service Area

Based in Songdo, Incheon, we support projects across Korea and work with overseas clients through drawings, 3D CAD, BOM data and remote engineering reviews.
