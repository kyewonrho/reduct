REDUCE PRODUCT TECHNOLOGY

Tooling should start after product geometry and production conditions are aligned.

We review moldability, design and manufacture injection tooling, and coordinate trial and production requirements with molding partners.

Product DFM for molding

Undercuts, parting lines, draft, wall thickness, ribs, bosses, fasteners and cosmetic requirements are reviewed before tooling.

Mold design and manufacturing

Tooling structure is defined around product size, resin, cavity count, expected volume, cycle target and quality requirements.

Trial and modification support

Trial parts are reviewed for dimension, warpage, assembly and appearance, followed by product or tooling corrections where necessary.

Injection production linkage

After tooling, molding production can be coordinated with partner factories according to volume and quality requirements.

Service Area

Based in Songdo, Incheon, we support projects across Korea and work with overseas clients through drawings, 3D CAD, BOM data and remote engineering reviews.
